# Voyc

> Voyc is a conversation intelligence and compliance monitoring platform for regulated industries (financial services, insurance). It monitors 100% of contact centre interactions with speech analytics AI, flags high-risk cases, complaints, and vulnerable customers in near real time, automates QA, and produces regulatory compliance evidence (including FCA Consumer Duty). Calls arrive from telephony platforms via SFTP or API; a Voyc API (v3) covers conversation uploads and data export, with a webhooks surface.
